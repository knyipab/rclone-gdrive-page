Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy describes these personal rclone and Google Drive setup notes at github.com/knyipab/rclone-gdrive-page. The site is a static set of pages for personal reference. It has no user accounts, contact form, analytics, or file-transfer feature.

Information handled by this website

This site does not ask visitors to submit personal information and does not receive Google passwords, Drive files, or rclone authorization tokens. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as request logs, to provide and secure its hosting service under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Using rclone with Google Drive

rclone is separate software that you install and run on your own machine. When you configure it to connect to Google Drive, it uses the Google account permissions you grant to perform the actions you request, such as listing, copying, or synchronizing files. rclone may store connection settings and authorization tokens in its local configuration. Files are transferred between Google Drive and the destination you select; this website is not involved and does not receive that content.

Review the rclone Google Drive documentation and rclone project information for details about the software. Google handles account data under its own Privacy Policy and applicable Google API policies.

Your choices

You choose whether to use rclone, which account permissions to grant, and what files and destinations to use. You can remove an application's Google account access in your Google Account connections, and you can delete rclone configuration from your own device. Removing access may stop configured transfers from working.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if these pages or their practices change. Questions about the pages can be raised through the repository's GitHub issues. Issues are public, so do not post private or sensitive information there.
